# Copper Dusk

Late-day copper on warm sand. Hospitable and a little theatrical, for work that is allowed to have a mood.

## Color Palette

- **Base — aged copper**: `#8a4b2a` — titles, section headers
- **Surface — warm sand**: `#faf3ea` — page or slide background
- **Body — burnt umber**: `#33261d` — running text
- **Accent — ember**: `#9c5227` — emphasis, pull quotes, decorative rules

## Typography

- **Headers**: Georgia
- **Body Text**: Verdana

## Best Used For

Brand and hospitality decks, event programmes, menus, portfolios, customer-facing storytelling.
