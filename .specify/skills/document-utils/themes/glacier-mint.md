# Glacier Mint

Ice white with a deep teal base. Clean and clinical without going sterile; the accent is cold enough to feel measured.

## Color Palette

- **Base — deep fjord**: `#14524f` — titles, section headers
- **Surface — ice white**: `#f1f7f6` — page or slide background
- **Body — cold slate**: `#1f2c2b` — running text
- **Accent — meltwater**: `#1f7268` — callouts, metrics, chart series

## Typography

- **Headers**: Calibri Light
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Clinical and laboratory reports, data-quality summaries, research findings, documentation that must read as neutral.
