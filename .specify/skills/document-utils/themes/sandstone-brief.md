# Sandstone Brief

Olive stone on unbleached paper. Understated and procedural: nothing in the palette asks to be noticed.

## Color Palette

- **Base — olive stone**: `#5c5340` — titles, clause headers
- **Surface — unbleached**: `#faf7f0` — page or slide background
- **Body — dry ink**: `#2b2a24` — running text
- **Accent — field olive**: `#5a6a36` — defined terms, cross-references, margin rules

## Typography

- **Headers**: Times New Roman
- **Body Text**: Arial

## Best Used For

Contracts, policies, legal and consulting briefs, compliance documentation, anything that must look conventional on paper.
