# Terracotta Ledger

Warm clay and ink on cream paper. The palette of a printed annual report: serious, but not cold.

## Color Palette

- **Base — fired clay**: `#7a3b2e` — titles, table header bands
- **Surface — cream paper**: `#fbf6ef` — page or slide background
- **Body — pressed ink**: `#2e2a26` — running text and figures
- **Accent — kiln orange**: `#a8563f` — totals, emphasis, rule lines

## Typography

- **Headers**: Constantia
- **Body Text**: Candara

## Best Used For

Financial models, annual and quarterly reports, audit summaries, long-form documents that will also be printed.
