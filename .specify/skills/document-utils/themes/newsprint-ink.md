# Newsprint Ink

Ink black on newsprint bone with one bulletin red. High contrast, no gradient, no mood — the palette of a front page.

## Color Palette

- **Base — press ink**: `#14161a` — headlines, mastheads
- **Surface — newsprint**: `#f7f5f0` — page or slide background
- **Body — column ink**: `#1b1d22` — running text
- **Accent — bulletin red**: `#b3261e` — corrections, alerts, the one thing to notice

## Typography

- **Headers**: Arial Black
- **Body Text**: Arial

## Best Used For

Announcements, release notes, incident bulletins, newsletters, posters and any document whose job is to be read fast.
