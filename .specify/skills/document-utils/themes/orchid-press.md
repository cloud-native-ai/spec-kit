# Orchid Press

Aubergine over bone, with a dusty orchid accent. Editorial weight: the palette of a book jacket rather than a slide template.

## Color Palette

- **Base — aubergine**: `#4b2347` — titles, chapter heads
- **Surface — bone**: `#f8f3f7` — page or slide background
- **Body — ink violet**: `#2c2230` — running text
- **Accent — dusty orchid**: `#8e4a7e` — pull quotes, emphasis, folios

## Typography

- **Headers**: Palatino Linotype
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Essays, catalogues, luxury and cultural programmes, invitations, long-form reading where typography carries the tone.
