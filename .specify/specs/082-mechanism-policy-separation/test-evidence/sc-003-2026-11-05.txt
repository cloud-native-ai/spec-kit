## 7. The ownership reading (second verbatim line of § 1)

`baseline=575 current=580 added=5 co_worktree=4 in_allowed=0 unregistered_additions=0
allowed_in_unit=10`, and `allowed_paths_present=10 unproven=none`.

- `added=5` = the worktree holds 5 in-unit paths the baseline does not: row 2 (this
  feature's registered new path) + the 4 `skills/external/ssh-utils/` paths the
  concurrent work line adds.
- `co_worktree=4` = the added paths that carry no 082 commit — the ssh-utils four.
  `in_allowed=0` records that they are *not* in this feature's allowed set; they are
  reported, never folded into "clean".
- `unregistered_additions=0` = no tracked, 082-attributed, unregistered addition.
- `allowed_paths_present=10 unproven=none` = every allowed path that exists on disk is
  tracked by git, so the claim cannot rest on a file another thread may delete.

## 8. Recomputing this file's argument from the pasted output alone

1. `unit=575` ← § 2's three `git ls-tree | wc -l` readings (548 + 27).
2. `compared=unit` ← both fields of the § 1 line are 575.
3. `allowed_in_unit=10` ← § 3 = GM-03 rows 1–8 + 19–20 (the table above).
4. `allowed_in_baseline=9` ← § 3: exactly one registered path (row 2) is new.
5. `complement=566` ← `575 − 9`, the § 1 line's own two fields.
6. `frozen_mismatches=0` ← the § 1 line; and § 6's empty difference on both sides says
   the same thing on the path side.
7. The negative direction is carried by the module's own `tmp_path` cases (one-byte
   change in the complement, unregistered new path, aggregate-scope word) — all four
   read `passed` in the same verbatim run.
