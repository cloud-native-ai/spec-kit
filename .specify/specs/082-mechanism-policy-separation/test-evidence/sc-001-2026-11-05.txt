## 1. The two judge invocations (verbatim prompts)

Judge A (cwd = a directory holding only `owner.md` and `questions.md`):

    qodercli -p --no-session-persistence --tools Read --permission-mode default \
      "读 owner.md（一份完整的规范文档）和 questions.md。只用 owner.md 的内容判断每个问题中的
       片段属于哪一层。每题只能给出一个答案，取值必须是 机制、策略、待澄清 之一。输出格式：
       每题一行，形如 Q-01: 机制 。不要解释，不要读写其它文件。"

Judge B (a second, independent process; its own copy of the two files):

    qodercli -p --no-session-persistence --tools Read --permission-mode default \
      "你是判定者 B。请阅读 owner.md（规范文档全文）与 questions.md。判断 questions.md 中
       每个片段应归入哪一层，依据只能来自 owner.md。每题给一个答案，取值必须是 机制、策略、
       待澄清。每题一行，格式为 Q-01: 机制 。不写解释，不读其它文件。"

Neither prompt names the three values' definitions, gives an example answer, or mentions
which layer is common — the only input that carries meaning is the owner document.

## 6. The reading

    judges_agree = 14 / 14 = 100.0 %

Both judges answered the identical value on every one of the 14 items, which is SC-001's
criterion ("结论一致率达到 100%"). Agreement is not scored against the key — the key is
printed only so a reader can see that the agreement is also correct, not agreement on a
common misreading.

## 7. Decidability findings

None between the two judges. No item produced divergent verdicts, so no item is reported
as undecidable. The boundary case (Q-06 / S-12, the paragraph that carries both layers)
was answered `待澄清` by both judges independently, which is the one value the owner's
§ Boundary Test reserves for "两个方向都成立" — the case that a prose-only reading is most
likely to collapse.

SC-001's criterion is agreement between two judges, and that is 100%. The separate
SC-005 reading on the same set (a third, uninvolved reader) is recorded in
`sc-005-2026-11-05.txt` and did produce one divergence from the owner's own answer;
that is a reading about a reader, not a second-judge disagreement, and it is not folded
into this criterion's rate.

## 8. Recomputing this reading

Re-run the two invocations of § 1 against the two files whose sha256 are quoted in the
header. The set is frozen by the snapshot; the owner copy is frozen by its hash; the
prompts are quoted verbatim. A different model or a later owner revision would produce a
new reading, which is what "one sample run" means here — it is evidence, not a gate.
