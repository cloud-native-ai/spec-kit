## Disclosed noise band

The trigger engine/promotion/tuning suites are order-sensitive in this tree: the union of their
failures moves by 3-6 names between otherwise identical runs. A NEW-failure reading inside those
modules must be re-confirmed by a second run before it is treated as a regression; outside them,
the serial-vs-serial delta above is explained by the overlap window alone.
