**In scope**: read-only snapshot aggregation, verdict delegation, independent completion, eight interruption annotations, stage artifact/commit evidence, watchbeat read, probe input, advisory recovery action, owner reference and docs. The slice FR definitions are FR-001 through FR-025; the success criteria are SC-001 through SC-008.

**Out of scope**: `recover`/`check-spine` execution, state-spine writes, recovery records, `attempts/` isolation, recovery lock, handoff files, lifecycle validation, live-process stop decisions, cross-slice test-matrix aggregation.
