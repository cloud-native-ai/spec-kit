## The conflict

Two recorded intents cannot both hold:

1. **FR-019 / C-09**: the live instructions MUST carry the ambient bullet (hence +277 B).
2. **SC-009 / feature-ref.md § Delivery surface**: the change set MUST stay inside the 24 declared
   paths, and that list **explicitly** places `scripts/bash/generate-instructions.sh` outside the
   delta. Its own documented remedy for a new ambient section is to raise
   `INSTRUCTIONS_BUDGET_BYTES` with a recorded reason.

This is a decision (two readings, no artifact picks one), not a correction — so this package stops
here instead of choosing unilaterally.
