**Why this is the feature's main flow and not a test run**: feature 087's main flow is *"a flow reports what it is
doing, and the framework can answer what is running right now"*. The chain below drives that flow in one pass on a
deterministic local fixture: the roster is judged for its reporter seat (the seat obligation), the digest renders a
bounded current-state response (the reachability floor), a supervised sub-flow is reported and judged against the
declared stall threshold (the master-reporting duty), and the deterministic guard returns a verdict over the
delivered artifacts. No network, no cluster, no LLM, no wall-clock dependence.
