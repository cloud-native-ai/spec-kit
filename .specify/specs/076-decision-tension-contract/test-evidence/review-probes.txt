Reading: `KEY_RE` (`scripts/python/validate-falsification-quartet.py:64`) anchors the four literal

rule. A SI-4 dispatch payload is a markdown document, so a bullet list is the ordinary shape — and
it is rejected as "no attempt", the worst available direction for a mechanism whose purpose is to
tell "ran" from "claims it ran".
