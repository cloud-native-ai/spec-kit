## Disclosure decisions

- The driver skill is edited at its framework source (`skills/external/...`) and the runtime tree under
  `.specify/skills/...` is regenerated by `scripts/python/sync-mirrors.py --write --only skills/external`;
  the mirrored pair is judged by the sync engine, never edited twice.
- `feedback-utils.py` is edited at its framework source `scripts/python/` and mirrored to
  `.specify/scripts/python/` by the same engine (`--only scripts`).
- No CI workflow is added; the fast tier is a runner default (requirements A10).
- Two pre-existing baseline failures (research B-4) are compared by name, never by count.
- `test_c20` (corpus byte identity) stays subprocess and cache-free; it is the write-detector.
- New evidence files are never written under `.specify/specs` while a corpus-scanning test is running
  (research B-5; hazard record `test-evidence/corpus-write-hazard.txt`); measurement output lands outside
  the corpus during runs and is copied afterwards.
- **Overlap adjudication**: `overlap-check.py` returns `decision=overlap` (recorded in
  `test-evidence/overlap-check-plan.txt`). Every relation is `same-path` or `nested-path` on shared
  infrastructure surfaces — the runner (same path as 086 and earlier runner features), `scripts/python`
  (directory of the engine and its siblings), the driver reference docs (same paths as 058/060/065/075).
  The adjudication is **additive coexistence**: 098 adds clauses/options only, reopens no 086 clause text,
  and changes no other feature's owned surface; the runner and the two driver references remain single-owner
  files edited by their owning chain.
