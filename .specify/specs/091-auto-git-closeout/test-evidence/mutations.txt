## Limit recorded, not papered over (R-21)

The retry arm above reds through the **run shape** ("stops at that step"), not through the ban
sentence. Measured: replacing `**no automatic retry**` with `**automatic retry**` leaves the
predicate GREEN, because the § No retry, no force section still contains the heading text
"No retry" and the sentence "It is never a retry mechanism" farther down. That is a real,
recorded limit of a structural read: it detects the *negative* wording the owner publishes, not
the polarity of every sentence in the section. The behavioural arms
(`test_a_rejected_push_stops_before_the_remote_changes`,
`test_a_merge_conflict_stops_before_any_push`) are what actually prove "no retry happened" —
they assert the remote's ref listing and the pushed set after a failure, which a retry would
change. The structural arm is the fast check, not the proof.

## Why all arms are in-memory

`closeout-verification.contract.md` C-1 separates the two evidence grades and this module's
header restates it: a structural predicate reads files, a behavioural predicate runs git against
a throwaway repository. Neither writes a tracked file, so a mutation cannot leave the tree in a
half-mutated state — the substitution is applied to a local string and discarded.

## R-21b — measured 2026-11-09 in the completion slice: the behavioural retry arm

The retry arm above mutates **wording**. T041's row text asks specifically that the T039 / T040
assertions (the two failure fixtures) go red when an automatic retry is injected. That is a
behavioural question, and slice 1 had no reading for it. Measured now with the driver's own
mutation hook (`run_closeout(..., attempt_retry=N)`), which retries the failing step N times:

Second measured limit, recorded rather than smoothed (this is the R-21b entry):

- the retry hook lives on the **push** step, so the **merge-conflict** arm is unchanged by it
  (`retries == 0` under both runs) and its guarantee stays "stops at the merge, no push after it".
  The test asserts that reading explicitly, so a later change that makes the merge retryable
  turns this arm red instead of silently passing.
- what changed in the module for this arm: `CloseoutOutcome.retries` (a reported field, never a
  constant) — a report field, not a second rule.
