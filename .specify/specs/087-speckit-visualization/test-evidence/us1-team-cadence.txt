## Part 3 — readability verdict (T019 ↔ C-31)

**Verdict**: `pass` — every captured US1 line reads without access to the run, the engine, or this feature's
vocabulary.

- **No internal engine identifier where a user-facing name exists**: the lines carry `structure=`, `run=`, `trigger=`,

  reader is looking at.
- **The facts needed to act are present**: which structure, which run, how far along, what changed since last time,
  how much is left, and whether anything blocks. A reader who is not in the run can tell a healthy run from a stuck
  one from `blocker=` plus `delta=` alone, without opening a file.
- **Everything else is reached by path reference**: the element *definitions* are not restated in the line or the
  renderer — they are owned once by `shared/guidelines/progress-reporting.md` § 汇报内容下限 and reached by anchor
  from `scripts/python/status-digest.py`'s docstring. The line names the labels; the owner defines them.
- **One deliberate reserve**: the `delay=` field appears only on a delayed tick. Its absence on an undelayed line is
  not a missing fact — an undelayed line has no delay to state, and the field's presence on the delayed line above
  keeps that distinction visible rather than inferred.
