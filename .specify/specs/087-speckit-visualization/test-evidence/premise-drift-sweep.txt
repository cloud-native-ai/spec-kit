# T046 — the premise drift sweep after implementation

**Capture date**: 2026-11-06T08:30Z (UTC)
**What this row is**: a read-only scan of the feature's own artifacts for **(a)** environment verdicts that
contradict the § Environment Prerequisites probes and **(b)** counts that no longer match the tree, followed by an
in-place correction of the stale side in the **same run**. It claims no clause and no new mechanism.
**Direction of correction**: where an artifact states a pre-change count and the artifact is a *measurement record*
(`research.md` RO-01, `quickstart.md`'s "actual output"), the pre-change reading is **left as the pre-change
measurement it is** and the post-change reading is added beside it — rewriting a dated measurement into today's
number would destroy the very baseline the feature diffs against. Where the artifact states a **planning premise**
(`plan.md` § Execution Premises, § Mirror Obligations), the stale figure is corrected in place and the correction is
annotated on the same row, per `/speckit.implement` step 8 § Gate-premise decay ("re-reference the premise, measure
the new form, annotate the falsified premise inline").
