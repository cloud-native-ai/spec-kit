## 3. Disclosures — why two `final` rows are `partial` and one `supporting` row is `not-measured`

1. **SC-001 — the run is a fixture run, and it is 4 s, not ≥90 s.** The criterion's subject is a team run
   "lasting more than 90 seconds". What was captured is a fixture run over the compliant roster that emitted
   three tick lines and two boundary lines across three **2-second** intervals (wall clock 4 s). Compressing the
   interval does not change **what** is being verified — the lines are emitted by the delivered renderer, not
   hand-written, and the attribution, four-element, profile and boundary behaviour are all visible in the capture —
   but it does not satisfy the stated measurement condition either. It is therefore `partial`, not `pass`, and the
   ladder in `verification.md` names the missing piece: an actual multi-minute team run.
2. **SC-002 — the population is empty, so the population half is measured on fixtures.** T003's name list records
   13 rosters, **13 outside the population, 0 in-population**, and that is a measurement of the tree rather than a
   policy choice. The guard's RO-04 correspondingly returns `not-evaluated` with that reason, which
   `contracts/status-digest.contract.md` C-46 requires ("never `pass`"). Both halves of the criterion —
   the defect side naming the seat, and the compliant side resolving to the shipped template — are captured on
   fixtures, and the 13-path sweep confirms no pre-existing roster is judged. That is a real measurement of the
   rule; it is not a measurement **over a populated tree at scale**, so the row is `partial`.
3. **SC-004 — the host evidence is a probe, not a per-host version record.** `us2-execution-modes.txt` records
   `qodercli` and `claude` as resolved on this machine (path probes), while `requirements.md` RS-003's joint
   acceptance asks for "宿主名 + 版本原文". The carrier/mode mapping — which is what SC-004 itself asserts — is
   fully captured for all three modes; the version string is not. Recorded here rather than raised to a defect of
   this criterion, because the criterion's own subject is the carrier, and disclosed so that no reader infers a
   version record that does not exist.
4. **SC-009 — `not-measured`, per the task text's explicit instruction, recorded as `blocked`.** No row above
   produces a real multi-minute dogfood run, and the task text fixes that reading: "SC-009 additionally carries the
   explicit reading `not-measured` unless a row of this feature produces a real multi-minute dogfood run, because
   no row above schedules one and a fixture run is not that evidence." **The recorded status in `verification.md`
   is `blocked`, not the string `not-measured`**: the status vocabulary is a closed six-value set
   (`pass | fail | partial | blocked | deferred | unknown`, owned by `shared/workflow/acceptance-hardening.md`
   § Verification Vocabulary) that no carrier may widen, and none of its members means "measured as unmade".
   `blocked` is the honest member — "the environment cannot measure this assertion, so the feature is NOT
   ACCEPTABLE" — and `unknown`/`deferred` would each misreport (the first says no status row exists, the second
   says not attempted but possibly fine). The two readings agree in substance: nothing was measured and nothing is
   claimed. It is `supporting`-path, so a non-`pass` status here does not by itself force `Blocked`; it does keep
   the end-to-end dogfood claim unmade. The ladder fields for this row are in `verification.md`.
5. **Why SC-010 is `pass` while SC-009 is not.** SC-010's evidence is a **machine reading over the delivered
   change set** that was captured in full (`cl-01-closure.txt` § 2–§ 3), including the honest disclosure of the two
   introduced render-parameter variables. SC-009's evidence would be a *run*, and no run exists. The difference is
   not severity — it is whether the capture exists at all.
