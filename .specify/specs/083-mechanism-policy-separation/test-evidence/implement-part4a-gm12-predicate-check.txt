## 3. Ruling (the scan's two vocabularies)

- **Class names** (E-10, read at call time): counted by **form** — table-row leading
  cell, definition-list member, or a token standing alone on its line. A bare adjective
  inside an English sentence is not an identifier. Every class is printed with its
  count (`class-name[<name>]=<n>`), so an abbreviated ruling is visible, not silent.
- **Class criteria** (FR-024's three distinctive conditions, literal): matched with
  whitespace and backticks normalized away. This is the vocabulary that carries no
  false-positive ledger, and it is what the criterion-copy negative case exercises.
