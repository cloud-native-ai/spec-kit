## 5. The reading

    correct = 13 / 14   (full set size = 14)

One item was answered differently from the owner's own answer: **Q-06**, the boundary
sample whose text carries a mechanism clause and a policy clause in one sentence
("重新生成镜像，然后把结果写进项目里"). The owner's answer is `待澄清`
(§ Boundary Test 3: when the sentence cannot be split and both readings hold, the item is
recorded as 待澄清 with the reason); the reader collapsed it to `策略`.

Recorded as it happened, not repaired:

- The count is 13/14, which is **below** the full set size, so SC-005's literal wording
  ("正确回答不少于 owner 附录给出的全部样例") is **not** met by this one reader sample.
- SC-005's path is `supporting`: the criterion is a supplementary reading, not a closure
  gate (`requirements.md` § SC-005's Path rationale). The decidability itself is carried
  by SC-001 (14/14 agreement) and by the parseability guard
  `tests/contract/test_mechanism_policy_decidability.py`.
- The failure mode is one of reading discipline, not of the closed set: the same item was
  answered `待澄清` by both SC-001 judges, so the value is reachable from the document.
  A reader who stops at "this sentence mentions a path, therefore policy" skips
  § Boundary Test's three-step rule; the document states that rule, and states that
  待澄清 is not a third decision value but a hold that must name which layer is the real
  constraint.
- No second reader was run to look for a passing sample. One reader is what the criterion
  names; running readers until one scores full marks would turn a reading into a search.
