implied by Q1's position. Before this stage the protocol said "the audit's primary object is the

`sync-mirrors.py --write`). This is a correction under fast-fail's test: exactly one reading fits the
recorded intent (C-6's own wording), it is local and reversible, and it falsifies no downstream
premise — the guard's leg 1 and the m3 mutant both exercise the sentence.
