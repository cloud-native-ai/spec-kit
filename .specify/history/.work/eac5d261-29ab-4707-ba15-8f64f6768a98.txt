## Two refinements I'd push back on / suggest

1. **Name.** `share/` reads like Unix `/usr/share` and is a bit generic. Consider **`.specify/shared/`** (clearer English) or a more descriptive **`.specify/references/`** / **`.specify/protocols/`**. Whatever you pick, the `workflow/` subfolder is good — it leaves room for other shared-reference families later. (Minor; your `share/workflow` is fine if you prefer it.)
2. **Reference form.** Keep the existing convention: **command templates use root-relative** paths (`share/workflow/...`) and let `_rewrite_paths` prefix `.specify/`; **skills that today hardcode `.specify/skills/sdd-workflow/...`** should hardcode `.specify/share/workflow/...`. Don't mix forms.

## Alternatives I considered (and why yours wins)
- **Leave as-is** — cheapest, but the semantic problem and accidental-invocation risk remain. Rejected.
- **Fold into `.specify/memory/`** — memory has bespoke per-file copy handling and is for *evolving project knowledge*, not static shared protocols. Mismatch.
- **Fold into `.specify/templates/`** — templates get copied wholesale already (least new plumbing), but "template" ≠ "protocol/reference," so it trades one naming lie for another.

A **dedicated shared-references tree** (your proposal) is the most honest and the most extensible.
