## Generation Basis

- The artifacts predate the current session and were authored across an unattended SDD loop; a same-author defect hunt was therefore delegated to two fresh-context read-only subagents with disjoint scopes (core FR-001…FR-012; boundary FR-013…FR-025 plus SC and cross-artifact delivery). The orchestrating pass validated every emitted anchor against `requirements.md` and the named downstream artifacts before writing an item.
- Depth is Standard (default; no override supplied). Audience is the framework maintainer / `sdd-loop-driver` maintainer who will decide whether the requirement text changes. Focus clusters were selected because they carry the highest downstream risk: an ambiguous interruption/action rule becomes a wrong recovery suggestion, and an incomplete output contract becomes a silent reader break.
- Items whose answer would require running the product, mutating files, or observing a test result were excluded by construction. A checked item means the requirement text has been corrected or a clarification decision has been recorded — not that a test passes.
